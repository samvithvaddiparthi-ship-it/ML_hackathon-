## Sampling  (fig04, fig05)
* Δt median: train 1 s, student_test 1 s; share of 1 s steps per trip: train median 99.1 % (min 53.1 %), student_test median 99.9 % (min 86.1 %).
* Gaps > 5 s: train 1216, student_test 44; gaps > 30 s: train 133, student_test 2.

## Speed and jumps  (fig06)
* Median implied speed: train 39.9 km/h, student_test 50.9 km/h.
* Steps > 60 m/s (216 km/h): 555 in 20 trips; worst: 00000032 [train] 365, 00000013 [train] 103, 00000001 [train] 52.

## GPS noise vs reference route  (fig07)
* All fixes pooled – train: median 5.6 m, p95 140.3 m; student_test: median 5.2 m, p95 16.8 m.
* The pooled train p95 is dominated by the truncated-route trips ['00000002', '00000033', '00000054'] (trip 54 alone has 19,313 fixes, most beyond the end of its route). Excluding them: train median 4.9 m, p95 17.6 m (this is what fig07 shows).
* Per-trip p95, median over trips: train 14.2 m, student_test 15.8 m → basis for the candidate search radius and the HMM emission σ.

## Geography and networks  (fig08–fig11)
* Trip centres span lon -100.3…137.2, lat -34.6…61.7, so distances use a per-trip azimuthal-equidistant projection.
* Network size: 649 – 307,392 arcs per trip (median 18,761).
